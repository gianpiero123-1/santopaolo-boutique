The nightly price is the figure that persuades, the total is the figure you pay. Between the two sit four charges that almost no platform shows together.

## What gets added

**Final cleaning.** A fixed amount per stay, so it weighs heavily on one or two nights and barely at all across a week. This is why apartments look expensive for short stays.

**Tourist tax.** Per person per night, collected by the property rather than the platform. Across six people for five nights it becomes a visible line.

**Platform commission.** Built into the price you see but paid by you regardless, in the form of a higher rate.

**Deposit.** Not a cost if it comes back, but it is money held. Ask the amount, the method and the return timeline.

## How to get the real number

One question: what do I pay in total, for these dates, for this many people, everything included.

A serious operator answers with a single figure and breaks it down. Anyone answering with a nightly rate and "we'll sort it later" is postponing a surprise.

<InPractice>
- Ask for the total with tourist tax included, not listed separately
- Check whether mid-stay cleaning is included on longer bookings
- On short stays the final clean weighs heavily, an extra night can be better value
- For groups, ask whether a whole-property rate exists instead of per-unit pricing
- Check what happens if you add a person after confirmation
</InPractice>
