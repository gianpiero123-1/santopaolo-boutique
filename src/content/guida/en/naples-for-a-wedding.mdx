A wedding is not a group holiday, it is an event with fixed timings. That changes entirely what the accommodation has to do.

## The three specific needs

**The group has to be together.** On the morning everyone gets ready, waits for each other and leaves together. Guests spread across three hotels means three departures to coordinate and somebody arriving late.

**You need space to get ready.** Make-up, hair, dresses, the photographer. A hotel room is neither large enough nor well lit. An apartment with a wide living room and big windows works far better, and it is where the getting-ready photographs happen.

**You need parking.** Guests travelling in come by car. If each of them has to find central parking on the morning of the wedding, the problem multiplies by the number of cars.

## What to ask

How many units share a building and across how many floors. Whether there is a large room where the group can gather. How many bathrooms there are in total, because that is the real constraint on the morning. Whether check in can be early or check out late, since wedding timings do not match standard ones. Whether there is parking on site.

And whether a whole-property rate exists, which across two or three nights with ten or more people changes the arithmetic.

<InPractice>
- Hold the accommodation before confirming invitations, not after
- Count bathrooms, on the morning of the event they are the bottleneck
- Check whether check out can move to the day after the event
- The photographer needs a room with natural light, ask which way it faces
- If guests drive in, on-site parking removes five separate problems
</InPractice>

## Why Vico Santa Maria a Cappella Vecchia works

Five units on a single floor, from 45 to 90 square metres, totalling 325 square metres and up to eighteen beds. The group uses one entrance and stays on one level, so nobody crosses the city on the morning of the event.

The largest unit, 90 square metres with two bedrooms and two bathrooms, works as the getting-ready base. The building's on-site garage handles guests arriving by car.

For bookings like this, direct contact is the route, since it allows every unit to be held together and non-standard arrival and departure times to be agreed.
