A launch, a client project, a building site, a temporary office for a quarter: when a company sends 4 to 20 people to Naples, the hard part is keeping them close. In Chiaia, at Vico Santa Maria a Cappella Vecchia 8b, five apartments on one floor house up to 20 colleagues, with 6 bathrooms and one point of contact for the whole team.

## Where can a team of 10 or 15 stay in Naples without splitting up?

In several apartments run by one operator in the same building, because almost no apartment in central Naples sleeps more than six.

At Vico Santa Maria a Cappella Vecchia 8b the team takes one or more of the five apartments on the same floor, 330 square metres in all. Arrivals, cleaning and requests go through one person for the whole stay rather than three different properties.

## How many colleagues get a room of their own?

Six colleagues get their own room: the five units have 5 bedrooms plus a studio. Past six people, rooms are shared or the sofa beds come into play.

- Apartment 1, 95 sqm, two bedrooms and two bathrooms, 6 guests
- Apartment 2, 60 sqm, one bedroom with en suite bathroom and sofa bed, 4 guests
- Apartment 3, 70 sqm, one bedroom and sofa bed, 4 guests
- Apartment 4, 60 sqm, one bedroom and sofa bed, 4 guests
- Apartment 5, 45 sqm, studio, 2 guests

Between colleagues, count rooms rather than beds. Six bathrooms for twenty people means just over three per bathroom on a working morning.

## Short stay or transitional lease, which does the team need?

It depends on length: under 30 days you book a short stay, from 1 to 18 months you sign a transitional lease.

The lease suits a temporary office or a project running a few months: one all inclusive monthly rent, the contract in the company's name, no Naples residency needed. You will need a company registration extract, the legal representative's ID and proof of the temporary need, such as an assignment letter or the client contract.

## What does the team get for working?

Wi-Fi in every apartment and, on request, workstations set up before you arrive.

Put the number of workstations in the request so they are ready on day one. There is no meeting room: for the whole team together, the largest space is the living room in Apartment 1, 95 sqm. If the team lives on video calls, ask for real download and upload speeds first.

## What is included over a stay of several months?

On a transitional lease the rent covers utilities, Wi-Fi, two full cleans a week, a weekly change of bed linen, air conditioning, the gym and the steam room.

There are no utility contracts to set up. Laundry with collection and return is extra, and so is a car or motorbike space in the building's garage, at a monthly rate reserved for tenants and subject to availability.

## How does the team get around from Chiaia?

On foot or by metro: Chiaia station on Line 6 is 5 minutes away, the Chiaia funicular 10.

Capodichino airport is 6.8 km away, between 20 and 30 minutes by taxi, and Molo Beverello about 10 minutes. Teams arriving with company cars or a van have Garage Santopaolo in the same building, with 3 metres of clearance and 24 hour access.

## What does housing a team cost and how do you get a proposal?

On a transitional lease the whole floor costs 27,500 euro a month over 6 months and 25,000 euro a month over 12, all inclusive; under 30 days you ask for a quote on your dates.

Smaller teams combine units: Apartments 3 and 4, for 8 people, cost 11,000 euro a month over 6 months. Send the request from the Mid-term rentals page with headcount, length and the reason for the trip. You get an answer within 24 hours, dealing directly with the owner.
